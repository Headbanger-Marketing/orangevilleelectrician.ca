Plan the light and the work that makes it possible

A lighting project may involve fixture replacement, new wiring points or a different control layout. The contractor needs to assess existing wiring, mounting support, equipment suitability and access. Fixture appearance alone does not establish compatibility with a dimmer or the suitability of a location. Share product information and the intended room use before installation is priced.

Prepare the scope for your Orangeville property

Gather the boxes, product sheets and fixture dimensions you already have, and note which rooms they are meant for. Photograph each ceiling or wall position from below, and mention whether the garage lighting shares a switch with the house.

Ask the contractor to check each fixture against the existing wiring and mounting before pricing, and to say which ones should be returned or held. Ask whether lighting can be done room by room without leaving any fixture unfinished.

What should the assessment establish?

The fixtures for this Orangeville job were bought before anyone assessed the wiring. The assessment confirms whether each one suits its location, its mounting support and its control. Every new point is marked either as one the existing wiring can serve or as one that would need new wiring. If the garage lights share a circuit with rooms in the house, the scope explains what that sharing means for the work. Each room then appears as a complete phase, listing the fixtures included and those set aside, and any unsuitable fixture is named before installation.

Ontario electrical safety

ESA says almost all electrical work needs a notification of work. If you hire someone for home electrical work, use a Licensed Electrical Contractor. Agree who files the notification and request the Certificate of Acceptance after ESA review.

Read the current ESA notification and inspection guidance, contractor hiring guidance. You can verify an electrical contractor with ESA before agreeing to work.

Questions before you agree to work

Is it a problem if I bought fixtures before the wiring was assessed?

It can be, because a fixture may need support, a control or a connection the existing point does not offer. Share the product sheets early so the contractor can say what suits, what needs changing and what to return.

Can lighting be done one room at a time across several budgets?

Yes, if each room is finished and documented before the next begins. The contractor can explain how switches and any shared circuit will work between phases, so a finished room does not depend on one still waiting.
